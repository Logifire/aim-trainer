## Choose Your Drill: Tracking vs. Flick Shot

Switch instantly between two game modes in the header.

### 1. Tracking Aim Trainer — Smooth Control & Muscle Memory
Keep your crosshair locked on a continuously moving target. This builds hand-eye coordination, smooth mouse control and time on target (TOT).

**Three movement patterns:**
- **Smooth Sine Wave** — fluid, predictable arcs. Best for warmup and learning control.
- **Zig-Zag Bounces** — sharp direction changes. Trains reactive corrections.
- **Reactive Shifts (Hard)** — random teleports. Breaks prediction and forces true reaction.

> Search: `tracking aim trainer`, `smooth tracking practice`, `how to improve tracking aim`

### 2. Flick Shot Trainer — Speed & Precision
Targets pop up at random positions and disappear quickly. Click them before they vanish. Great for entry fraggers, snipers and anyone who whiffs flicks under pressure.

- Higher **Speed** = shorter lifetime + faster spawns
- Miss? It counts. Accuracy and avg. reaction time (ms) tracked every session.

> Search: `flick aim trainer`, `flick shot practice`, `reaction time trainer`

## How to Get Better Aim Fast — 3-Step Routine

Copy what good aim coaches use:

1.  **Warm up (2 min)** — `Large (80px)` + `Smooth` + `0.4×–1.0× Slow`. Focus on staying centered, not speed.
2.  **Build precision (5 min)** — `Medium (55px)` + `1.0×–2.0×` + `Zig-Zag`. Try to keep 60%+ Time on Target.
3.  **Push limits (3 min)** — `Small (32px)` + `Reactive` + `2.0×–3.5× Max` or `Flick Shot` at `2.0×`. This is where growth happens.

**Pro tip:** Chase **Time on Target** over score. TOT measures real mouse control. Check `Recent Training Sessions` (`30s • Reactive • 2.0× • 55px`) to see if you're actually improving day-to-day.

## FAQ — For Gamers Searching

**What is an aim trainer?**
An aim trainer is a practice tool that simulates FPS aiming drills — like tracking a moving target or flicking to random targets — so you can improve mouse accuracy, muscle memory and reaction time without launching a full game.

**Is this aim trainer free?**
Yes. 100% free in the browser. No ads, no paywall, no account. Open source under GPL-3.0.

**Do I need to download anything?**
No. It runs instantly at https://logifire.github.io/aim-trainer/ on any modern browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Safari).

**Can this help me in Valorant / CS2 / Apex / Overwatch?**
Yes. Tracking drills help with following moving enemies (Tracer, Jett strafes, Apex sliding). Flick drills help with peeks, entry frags and sniping. Many top players warm up 5–10 minutes in an aim trainer before ranked.

**What's the difference between tracking and flicking?**
Tracking = keeping your crosshair on a moving target (smooth control). Flicking = snapping your crosshair to a new target quickly (speed + precision). This trainer lets you practice both.

**How do I improve my aim fastest?**
Short, daily practice (10 min) beats one long weekly session. Use the 3-step routine above, prioritize accuracy over speed, and increase speed only when your accuracy stays stable.

**Does it work on mobile / touch?**
Yes — pointer events are supported, but a mouse is strongly recommended for meaningful FPS aim practice.
